Setup Guide

A quick walkthrough to get your Trading Journal Pro up and running in Notion.

01

Get your duplication link

After checkout on Lemon Squeezy you'll receive an email with a Notion duplication link. Check your inbox (and spam) — it should arrive instantly. If you haven't received it, drop me a line at jayvinn.pacheco@gmail.com.

02

Duplicate into your workspace

Click the duplication link. Notion will open a preview — hit Duplicate in the top-right corner. The full journal copies into your personal workspace (or team workspace, if that's where you clicked). This is your private copy — no shared editing conflicts.

03

Familiarise yourself with the layout

The journal is organised into views: Trade Entry (add new trades), Dashboard (aggregated stats and win-rate), and Daily / Weekly / Monthly P&L roll-ups. Spend two minutes clicking around — each view pulls from the same underlying trade database, so your data is always in sync.

04

Duplicate the Trade Entry page

The main Trade Entry database is already set up for new rows. No template work needed — just open the entry view and start logging trades. Each field maps cleanly: Ticker, Setup Type, Entry / Exit, Risk ($), Outcome ($ / R:R), Date, and Notes.

05

Set your risk parameters

Before logging real trades, confirm your risk-per-trade is entered in the Risk field consistently. The R:R calculations rely on it. Pick a percentage of your account (most traders use 1% or 2%) and stick with it — journal integrity depends on accurate risk data.

06

Check the dashboard populated

After logging at least one trade, hop to the Dashboard view. You should see win-rate, average R:R, and your best / worst sessions spinning up automatically. If nothing is showing up, ensure the trades have the correct date and that the dashboard view filters haven't been accidentally narrowed.

07

Link daily charts and screenshots

Use Notion's embed block or image upload inside each trade row for chart screenshots at entry and exit time. Being able to visually revisit your chart structure later is one of the journal's biggest advantages — you'll spot patterns you missed while trading live.

08

Archive finished trade rows

Once a trade is closed and reviewed, do not delete it — archive the row. Archiving keeps it queryable in dashboard roll-ups without cluttering your active entry list. Right-click a row in the database → Archive.

09

Schedule a weekly review

Set a recurring calendar reminder for every Friday close. During your review: check win-rate by setup, compare daily P&L streaks, and note any emotional or structural patterns. The journal rewards consistent review — the spreadsheet is only as good as what you extract from it.
